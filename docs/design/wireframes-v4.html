SeaBed Lens

Design system and wireframes, version 4. A calm, precise window into underwater computer vision research: the organisms, the model, the result and the research carry the page, and the interface stays out of the way.

  • Quieter type. Inter for the interface, Source Serif 4 for page titles and claim headlines only, JetBrains Mono only for IDs and code. Ordinary labels like Source, Test and Dataset are no longer mono.
  • Ocean teal leads. Teal is the brand and every primary action. Amber is now rare: the running indicator and nothing decorative.
  • Fewer layers. Page, content surface and emphasis. Tables, the inspector, About and the principles sit on the page with hairlines and whitespace, not inside cards. No default shadows. The hero gradient is gone.
  • Public navigation: Explore, Try it, Results, About, with GitHub and Thesis on the right. Benchmarks moved into Results; the Component Lab is internal, reached from Try it.
  • Home is image first: a short serif line, then the comparison, then one action and the sample images.
  • Controls recede. Underline tabs instead of segmented pills, text buttons for secondary actions, the image cleared of floating toolbars.

Quiet Scientific

Somewhere between a scientific publication, modern software and a field instrument. Visitors should leave thinking about the organisms, the model, the result and the research, and almost never about the UI. Six questions decide whether a component or page is ready.

  1. Can I remove something without losing information or function?If yes, remove it.
  2. Does this need a card?If whitespace or type can group it, it doesn't.
  3. Is this label necessary?If the context already says it, drop it.
  4. Does it look like a generic AI dashboard?If yes, simplify it.
  5. Does the interface compete with the imagery?If yes, simplify it.
  6. Does the page still work with all decoration removed?If yes, the hierarchy is sound.

Avoid: generic AI

Purple-blue gradients, glowing borders, glass panels, gradient text, sparkle icons, futuristic copy.

Avoid: SaaS dashboard

Card grids, giant metric tiles, badge clutter, nested cards, chrome around simple information.

Avoid: developer tool

Mono everywhere, terminal surfaces, tiny labels, more technical IDs than the reader needs.

Avoid: dated academic site

Dense paragraphs, Times-like type, paper clutter, footnotes on every sentence.

Grounded in how people see

The visual layer changed in v4; these findings did not. Each still produces a rule, and if a later choice conflicts with one, the finding wins.

FindingRule on SeaBed Lens
Chromatic adaptationThe eye adapts to the surround, so a tinted frame shifts how an image's colours look (the reason for ISO 3664's neutral surround).The well behind every image is neutral grey #17191B. Teal chrome stays one step further out, and the teal action colour never appears inside the image.
Positive polarityDark text on light is read more accurately (Piepenbrock et al., 2013).Results, Explore and About are light by default. Try it and the Lab are dark, like a photo editor, because there the image is the content.
HalationPure white on black blurs for readers with astigmatism.Dark-surface text is #E9EFED, never #FFF; light-surface text is #17211F, never black.
Position is decoded bestCleveland & McGill (1984).Dot plots on one shared axis with direct labels. No pie, radar, 3D or truncated bars.
Pre-attentive pop-outTreisman & Gelade (1980).Inside the image, hue means class and nothing else. Selection dims everything else. Teal marks the one primary action per view.
Change blindnessSimons & Rensink (2005).Compare in one place: swipe on Home, hold-to-flicker ground truth in Try it, swipe and difference views in the Lab.
Working memory, about 4 chunksCowan (2001).The inspector shows the count, threshold and top three classes; everything else expands on request.
Fitts's lawDistance and size set the time to hit a target.32px targets with a pointer, 44px on touch, controls low on phones.
Response-time limits0.1 s, 1 s, 10 s (Miller, 1968; Card et al., 1991).Filters re-render in under 0.1 s without animation. Runs keep the old result dimmed and show the step and elapsed time.
Peripheral motion captures attentionAbrams & Christ (2003).Nothing moves unless the visitor caused it. Motion only reports a state change.

Light

Dark workspace, fixed in both themes

Contrast rules the palette needs

  • Secondary #66716D is the lightest text: 4.75:1 on the page. Not on Subtle, where it drops to 4.45.
  • Muted #8A9591 never carries text (2.9:1). It is the boundary for inputs and secondary buttons on white (3.09:1), placeholders and disabled states.
  • Amber never carries text on light (2.07:1). Use it as a fill under dark text (7.96:1) or as a dot beside a label.
  • Dark Muted #74827E is for disabled states only; on the workspace surface it falls to 4.3:1.
  • Info blue is 4.17:1 on white, so it pairs with an icon or sits at 18px and up.

Shape and space

  • Radius: tags 5, controls and inputs 7, cards 10, image wells 12. No pill-shaped controls.
  • Borders: 1px #D9DEDC light, #263536 dark, for structure only. No nested bordered boxes.
  • Shadow: none by default. Popovers, menus, tooltips and the swipe handle only.
  • Spacing: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96. Sections 64–96, related content 16–24, control groups 8–12.

Overlay palette, 20 classes

High-luminance hues kept away from the water's blue-green, assigned by class frequency. Masks fill at 30% with a 1.5px outline and a dark halo; every mask can show a text label because 20 hues cannot all be told apart. Labels show for the top three and for whatever is hovered. A colour-blind preset sits under More options.

Built on shadcn, restyled until it no longer looks like shadcn. Each component says what it does, whether it is active and whether it can be used, and nothing more.

    Rolf Genree L. GarcesBS Computer Science · University of the Philippines Tacloban · 2026Adviser: John Paul T. Yusiong, PhD

    Paper (PDF) →Code →Dataset →

    Abstract

    Use the updated abstract that describes the deployed model (colour stems, convex fusion, no DAPFT). The September 2026 abstract describes the proposal-era design.

    How it works

    Each image is processed three ways for colour, then read by a ConvNeXt and a DINOv2 vision transformer side by side. Their features are blended at every scale, and two separate heads predict boxes and masks.

    Motion

    Motion reports a state change and never delays a scientific interaction. Only transform and opacity animate, with --ease-out (0.23, 1, 0.32, 1). Under reduced motion, movement is dropped and fades shorten.
